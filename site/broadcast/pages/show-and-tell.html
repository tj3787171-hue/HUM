The gist

HUM is a lab, not a raid. We built a SQL login, a simple circuit blog, and isolation math that keeps VNC on loopback display zones. Guest disk is /dev/vda. Host map is /dev/sda. You are getting that virtio disk off the ground into the cloud. I broadcast whatever you drop into the LVM-shaped cache, over HTTPS, with a public cert anyone on the housing can pin.

Dark-web entity lists and pentest attach scenes are out of scope. Do not spend time there. Do not wire VNC to /dev/nbd0.

How we connect

  1. You write files onto the virtio disk / LVM cache (or set HUM_LVM_CACHE).
  2. Run python3 scripts/hum_https_broadcast.py build. That copies login/circuit snapshots plus this show-and-tell into the cache.
  3. Run python3 scripts/hum_https_broadcast.py cert then serve. Bind stays 127.0.0.1:8443 unless you change it on purpose.
  4. I keep the frontend searchable: /search.html and /search.json?q=. That is the internet-facing index of the housing, not a crawl of the rest of the net.
